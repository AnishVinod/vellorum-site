👶 How the Stock Market Works (In Plain English) Beginner Blueprint
1. What is a Stock?

Imagine your friend opens a lemonade stand for $100. If you give them $10, you own 10% of the stand. That tiny slice of ownership is a stock share.

2. Why Do Prices Move?

It's just supply and demand. If it's 40°C outside and 100 people want lemonade, the price jumps. If it rains and nobody wants it, the price drops. Buyers push prices up; sellers pull them down.

3. How Do You Win?

Buy when prices are low or quiet, and sell when prices rise. Never put all your eggs in one basket—hold different types of assets so one drop doesn't wipe you out.
